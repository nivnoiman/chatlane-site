Chatlane

A light CRM layer for your chats on WhatsApp Web

Chatlane adds a panel beside your conversations: give each chat a Status (a lane it moves along), add Tags, set one Reminder, and keep a private Note. A Due tab brings back the chats that need you today.

  • Statuses and Tags you define, with presets for sales, support and personal use
  • One Reminder per chat, with notifications and a toolbar badge
  • Search across chats, tags and notes
  • English and Hebrew, including right-to-left
  • No account, no server: your data stays in your browser and your own Google sync
