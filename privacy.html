Chatlane

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Chatlane is a Chrome extension that adds Statuses, Tags, Reminders and Notes to chats on WhatsApp Web. Your data never leaves your browser and your own Google sync. The developer runs no server and receives no data.

What Chatlane reads

On web.whatsapp.com only, Chatlane reads which chat is open (its identifier, its display name, and whether it is a group) and the list of chats shown, so it can attach your Status, Tags, Reminder and Note to the right chat and draw markers in the chat list. It does not read, copy or store the content of your messages.

What Chatlane stores

DataWhere
Statuses, Tags, Reminders, chat identifiers and display names you tagChrome sync storage (chrome.storage.sync). Chrome syncs it across your own signed-in browsers through your Google account. The developer cannot see it.
NotesThis browser only (IndexedDB). Notes are not synced.
Settings and languageChrome sync storage.

If Chrome sync is off, everything stays on this device.

What Chatlane does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking
  • No network requests to the developer or to any third party
  • No selling, sharing or transferring of user data
  • No use of data for creditworthiness or lending purposes, and no human reading of your data

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
storage, unlimitedStorageKeep your Statuses, Tags, Reminders and Notes. Notes can grow beyond the default quota.
alarmsFire Reminders at the time you set.
notificationsShow a notification when a Reminder is due.

Your control

You can export your data to a file, import it back, or delete it: remove tags and notes in the panel, or uninstall the extension to remove local data. Data in Chrome sync can be cleared from the Chrome sync settings of your Google account.

Children

Chatlane is not directed at children.
